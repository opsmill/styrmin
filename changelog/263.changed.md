Changed backups to fail when Velero did not back up a volume that a component's backup strategy names, such as a `hostPath` volume from the `local-path` provisioner, which Velero skips with only a warning. Such a backup used to be marked completed without that volume's data. The component's step and the backup's error name the volume, and a failed backup hook is reported with the command that prints its output from Velero's log.
