Changed `styrminctl deployments apply --wait` and the SDK's `deployments.apply(wait=True)` and `apply_files(wait=True)` to wait up to 600 seconds instead of 300, so a deployment whose release fails can report the failed step instead of `wait timeout`. The wait still ends with `wait timeout` when the limit expires before a step fails, for example when the hooks of a deployment take longer than about 5 minutes. Added `--max-wait` to `deployments apply` and `max_wait` to the SDK methods to set another limit.
