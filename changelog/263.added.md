Added the `neo4j-community` backup kind for Neo4j Community Edition, which has no online backup: Styrmin pauses Neo4j while Velero copies its data volume, then resumes it, and a restore needs no hook. Neo4j does not answer queries while the backup runs, and a backup that runs longer than the one-hour backup timeout fails. The `neo4j` kind still requires Neo4j Enterprise Edition. A driver that sets `volumes`, `database_name` or `credentials_secret` on a `neo4j-community` component fails to load.
