Deleting a deployment now deletes its service aliases in the namespaces of the other deployments of its environment, and a create, update or upgrade of another deployment that runs during the deletion no longer writes them again or creates the deleted deployment's namespace again. A change of another deployment no longer fails when the deleted deployment's namespace is being deleted. If deleting an alias fails, the deletion continues and the alias can stay. Aliases left by deletions made before this version stay.
