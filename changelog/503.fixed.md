Fixed the service aliases that a deleted deployment left in the namespaces of the other deployments of its environment. Deleting a deployment through Styrmin, from the UI, `styrminctl` or the SDK, now deletes its service aliases, so the names of its services stop resolving in the other applications. A create, update or upgrade of another deployment that runs during the deletion no longer fails while the deleted deployment's namespace is being deleted, and no longer creates that namespace again. These aliases are not deleted: aliases left by deletions made before this version; an alias whose deletion failed, which the operator logs; the aliases of a deployment whose namespace was deleted directly, for example with `kubectl delete namespace`; the aliases of a `StyrminDeployment` annotated `styrmin.io/skip-teardown`, whose source deployment keeps running; and an alias that a change of another deployment wrote at the end of the deletion. A deployment that is created or changed while another deployment of its environment is being restored gets no aliases to it until a later change of either deployment. See [Service aliases](https://styrmin.pages.dev/docs/concepts-and-architecture/ingress-and-networking#service-aliases) to find and delete the aliases that stay.
