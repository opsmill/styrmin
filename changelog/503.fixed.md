Deleting a deployment now deletes its service aliases in the namespaces of the other deployments of its environment. A create, update or upgrade of another deployment that runs during the deletion skips those aliases, deletes any of them that it wrote as the deletion started, and no longer creates the deleted deployment's namespace again. A change of another deployment no longer fails when the deleted deployment's namespace is being deleted. If deleting an alias fails, the deletion continues and the alias can stay. Aliases left by deletions made before this version stay.
