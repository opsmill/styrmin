Fixed restores that failed after 10 minutes with `Namespace <namespace> was not deleted within 600s`, and application deployments and dedicated ingress controllers that stayed `DELETING`: when a namespace was deleted while the operator was checking the `StyrminDeployment` in it, the operator could keep the custom resource's finalizer, so the namespace stayed `Terminating`. When the upgraded agent starts, it removes a finalizer that an earlier agent kept, and Kubernetes finishes deleting the namespace, so you do not need to annotate the custom resource.
