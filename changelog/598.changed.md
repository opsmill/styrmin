Changed the meaning of `scope: external` for a service of a driver (breaking change): Styrmin now builds one Ingress for each external service of kind `http` or `https`, so a driver that declares a service `external` and also renders its own Ingress for it gives that service two Ingresses on the same host. The effect starts when a deployment runs this Styrmin release, not only when its driver version changes, so a deployment of a driver version that renders its own Ingress for an external service, such as `generic-app` version 1, gets the second Ingress at its next reconcile. Remove the Ingress from the chart values of the driver, or declare the service `internal`. The `infrahub` (version 6), `infrahub-enterprise` (version 5) and `generic-app` (version 2) example drivers changed in this release, and the `task-manager` service of both Infrahub drivers is now external. How many drivers outside this repository are affected is not known. See [Migrate a driver whose external service renders its own Ingress](https://styrmin.pages.dev/docs/drivers/migrating-external-service-ingress).
