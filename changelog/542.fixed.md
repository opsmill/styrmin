Fixed a restore and an environment clone leaving the deployment's namespace without its `styrmin/environment_id` and `styrmin/deployment_id` labels. A network policy that selects namespaces by them, such as the one that lets a shared ingress controller reach the application, no longer matched the namespace, so the restored application was unreachable while Styrmin showed it running. The restore now puts the labels back, and a clone gets the labels of its own environment and deployment. ([#542](https://github.com/opsmill/styrmin/issues/542))
