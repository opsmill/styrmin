Added service ingress overrides. A deployment can set the ingress class, the FQDN suffix, or both, for one service that its driver declares `scope: external`. The override goes in a manifest (`spec.config.services`), in the `services` field of the GraphQL config input (`ServiceConfigInput`), or in the Settings tab and the deploy form. The other services of the deployment keep the resolved class and suffix. An overridden Ingress carries only the ingress class: the `ip_whitelist` of the deployment and the external DNS record do not apply to it, and the application address is not on it. Styrmin refuses an override on a service that Styrmin builds no Ingress for (an internal service, or an external service of kind `tcp` or `udp`) or that the driver version does not declare (`SERVICE_INGRESS_NOT_BUILT`, `UNKNOWN_SERVICE_INGRESS_OVERRIDE`). Styrmin also refuses a driver version change that would remove the service, make it internal, or make its kind `tcp` or `udp` while an override is stored (`SERVICE_INGRESS_OVERRIDE_WOULD_BE_DROPPED`). Each error names the service. A manifest that sets `spec.config` keys and no `services` clears the stored overrides, and `styrminctl deployments apply` prints a `warning:` line that names the services. See [Service ingress overrides](https://styrmin.pages.dev/docs/concepts-and-architecture/service-ingress-overrides).
