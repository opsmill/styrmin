Added service ingress overrides: a deployment can set the ingress class, the FQDN suffix, or both, for one service that its driver declares `scope: external`, in a manifest (`spec.config.services`), through the `services` field of the GraphQL config input (`ServiceConfigInput`), or in the Settings tab and the deploy form, and the other services of the deployment keep the resolved class and suffix. The `ip_whitelist` of the deployment does not apply to an overridden service. Styrmin refuses an override on a service that Styrmin builds no Ingress for (an internal service, or an external service of kind `tcp` or `udp`) or that the driver version does not declare (`SERVICE_INGRESS_NOT_BUILT`, `UNKNOWN_SERVICE_INGRESS_OVERRIDE`), and a driver version change that would remove the service, make it internal, or make its kind `tcp` or `udp` while an override is stored (`SERVICE_INGRESS_OVERRIDE_WOULD_BE_DROPPED`), with an error that names the service. See [Service ingress overrides](https://styrmin.pages.dev/docs/concepts-and-architecture/service-ingress-overrides).
