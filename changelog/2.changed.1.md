Changed the operator's default readiness timeout (`STYRMIN_OPERATOR_DEPLOYMENT_READY_TIMEOUT`) from 300 to 270 seconds, below FluxCD's 5-minute HelmRelease timeout, so a rollout that fails because its pods cannot start (for example `ImagePullBackOff`) lists those pods and their reasons before FluxCD's rollback removes them. A rollout that becomes ready between 270 seconds and 5 minutes now shows `FAILED`, then `RUNNING`.
