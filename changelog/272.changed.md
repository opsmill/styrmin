Changed driver loading to reject an invalid `spec.info` block: loading a driver now fails when `spec.info.icon` is not an absolute `http` or `https` URL, or when a `spec.info.links` entry does not have exactly a `link` label and an `http` or `https` `url`. Before upgrading, replace placeholder values such as `icon: xxxx` in your driver specification files with a real logo URL, or remove the `icon` line.
