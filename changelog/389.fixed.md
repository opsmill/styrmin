Fixed a deployment reading `running` instead of `restoring` while a restore of it is in progress: an agent status report sent before Velero finished replaced the `restoring` state, so the guards that refuse other operations on a restoring deployment did not apply during that time. A restore whose workflow could not be dispatched is now marked `failed`, together with its deployment, instead of staying `pending` and keeping the deployment in `restoring`. A second restore of a deployment is now refused while a restore of it into another namespace is still in progress.
