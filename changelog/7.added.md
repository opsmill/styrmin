Added environment-level backup and restore: one action snapshots an environment — its config plus the declared state and a constituent backup of every eligible deployment — and one action restores it in place. Available as the `createEnvironmentBackup`, `restoreEnvironmentBackup` (ADMIN, since it rewrites the environment's config) and `deleteEnvironmentBackup` mutations plus the `environmentBackup(s)` / `environmentRestore(s)` queries, `client.environment_backups` in the SDK, the `styrminctl env-backups` command group, and a **Backups** tab on the environment detail page. An environment backup's status is derived from its constituents and adds a `PARTIAL` outcome; existing per-deployment backups are unaffected.
