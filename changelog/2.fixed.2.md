Fixed a restore of a Velero backup that no longer exists waiting until the restore timeout (two hours by default): Velero's `FailedValidation` phase now ends a backup or a restore at once, and the "Run the Velero restore" step and the restore's error message show Velero's validation errors. A backup or restore that Velero ends without a failure reason, such as a `PartiallyFailed` backup, now has the error of its failed step as its error message instead of an empty one.
