Fixed environment restores not changing the environment's dedicated ingress controller: when the restored config turns `dedicated_ingress` on, changes its values or turns it off, Styrmin now creates, redeploys or removes the controller as an environment update does, and redeploys the applications that are not restored onto the controller once it is `RUNNING`. A controller that the restore creates is attributed to the administrator who restores the backup. The restore no longer redeploys a deployment that is already at the restored config.
