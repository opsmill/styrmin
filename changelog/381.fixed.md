Fixed driver actions failing with "Authentication is required" when they call `stop_components`, `start_components` or `upgrade_deployment_version`. Upgrades of bundled-driver deployments ended with a failed task, and the steps after the first call (such as the Infrahub database migration) did not run. Each reconcile now gives its actions a short-lived token for the built-in `styrmin-actions` user and revokes the token when the reconcile ends.
