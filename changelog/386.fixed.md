Fixed a deployment that sometimes ran the configuration of an earlier change after two changes made one after another, on the deployment page, with `styrminctl` or through the API. Each change starts its own workflow, and the workflow of the earlier change could write the `StyrminDeployment` last. A deployment whose older configuration could not start then stayed `pending`. Styrmin now numbers each deployment's instances in the order it saves them and records the number on the `StyrminDeployment`, in the `styrmin.io/instance-sequence` annotation. A workflow leaves a `StyrminDeployment` that holds a higher number unchanged, and the task of the earlier change ends as `superseded`.
