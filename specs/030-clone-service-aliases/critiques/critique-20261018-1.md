# Critique Report: A Clone Writes Its Own Service Aliases and Restores None of the Source's

**Date**: 2026-10-18
**Feature**: [spec.md](../spec.md)
**Plan**: [plan.md](../plan.md)
**Verdict**: ⚠️ PROCEED WITH UPDATES

---

## Executive Summary

The spec states a real defect, recorded with E2E output on #530, against a documented promise (applications in a clone reach each other in the clone). The plan uses Velero's own `labelSelector` to leave the aliases out of the cross-target restore, and reuses the alias step of #503, so no new mechanism is added. One plan decision does not hold: after a failed alias write, the plan marks the counterpart's StyrminDeployment failed so that the deployment stays `FAILED`, but the operator's rollout of the recovered spec can run after that write and the status reporter then reports `RUNNING` (E1). The fix is to rely on the signals that last, the failed restore and the clone entry, and to stop promising a `FAILED` deployment. The other findings are small additions to the documentation and the tests.

### Failure Mode Analysis
| ID | Severity | Finding | Suggestion |
|----|----------|---------|------------|
| E1 | 🎯 | Research R-004 calls `mark_failed` on the counterpart's StyrminDeployment after a failed alias write, so that the deployment stays `FAILED`. The recovery creates the counterpart's StyrminDeployment with the source's kopf annotations, so kopf runs the update handler for the changed spec (`_roll_out_spec_update`, `operator/handlers/styrmin_deployment.py` L485-L540). That handler sets `displayState` to `Deploying` and the timer moves the deployment to `Ready`. When it runs after `mark_failed`, which the alias step's few seconds allow, it replaces the failed status, and the status reporter reports `RUNNING`. FR-004 and user story 3 scenario 1 then promise a `FAILED` deployment that the code does not keep. | Do not call `mark_failed` after an alias failure. The restore sets the counterpart to `FAILED`, as for any failed restore, and the deployment's status then follows its StyrminDeployment, whose workloads run. The signals that last are the restore (`FAILED`, with the error and the failed step) and the clone (`PARTIAL`, the entry `failed` with the reason). Update spec FR-004 and user story 3 scenario 1, research R-004, the plan's Design, the data model and the contract. |
| E3 | 💡 | A Kubernetes refusal of one alias fails the whole step, and the other aliases of the list may be left unwritten. The repair (R-005) writes them all again, so this is acceptable, but the plan does not say it. | Say in R-004 that the step stops at the first refused write, as in the create, update and upgrade flows, and that the repair writes the full set. |
